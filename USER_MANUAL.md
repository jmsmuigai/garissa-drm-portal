# Garissa County GEWAS User Manual

Welcome to the **Garissa County Disaster Risk Management (DRM) Early Warning System (GEWAS)**. This manual provides a quick guide to understanding what this system is about and how to use its features effectively.

---

## 🌍 What is GEWAS?
GEWAS (Garissa Early Warning & Adaptation System) is an advanced, AI-powered spatial analytics dashboard designed to monitor, predict, and mitigate disaster risks in Garissa County. 

Specifically built in response to the Super El Niño threat of 2026, the system integrates:
- **Historical Flood Data (UNOSAT)**
- **Real-Time Satellite Feeds (Google Earth Engine)**
- **Artificial Intelligence (Google Gemini)**

Its primary goal is to provide **decision support** to government officials, humanitarian partners, and local communities by mapping the vulnerability of schools, health facilities, water resources, and populations.

---

## 🚀 Key Capabilities & Navigation

### 1. Interactive Hazard Maps
The left sidebar gives you access to various specialized portals. 
- Click on **Master DRM Map** to view an integrated map with all active hazard layers.
- Check **Infrastructure Risk** maps (Schools, Health Clinics, Boreholes) to see exactly which critical facilities are exposed to flooding.

### 2. Version History
To maintain a clear track of how the portal has evolved, you can access historical snapshots via the **Version History** section in the sidebar.
- **v1.0 Prototype**: The early layout and structure.
- **v2.0 Beta**: The intermediate version featuring new analytics cards and styling.
- **v3.0 Final**: The current, fully operational dashboard with 3D logos, AI reports, and real-time telemetry.

### 3. Generate Well-Analyzed Reports
At the top right of the map toolbar, you will find a **📄 Generate AI Report** button. 
- Clicking this triggers the underlying Gemini AI engine to process the current map telemetry and generate a comprehensive, actionable advisory document (e.g., `AI_FLOOD_RISK_ADVISORY.md`).
- This report includes predictive timelines, exposed population metrics, and recommended mitigation actions tailored to the current data.

### 4. Live Telemetry Feed
When viewing the Master Map or Infrastructure maps, clicking on any specific marker (like a school 🏫 or clinic 🏥) will stream detailed vulnerability metrics to the **Asset Telemetry Feed** box in the bottom left of the sidebar. This allows you to quickly assess the risk level of individual assets.

### 5. Download Center
Need to use the data offline or in professional GIS software like QGIS? The **Download Center** section in the sidebar provides direct links to export `GeoJSON`, `KML`, and `CSV` files containing the risk-assessed data.

## 🎨 Personalization
The system is equipped with a theme switcher (🎨 **Portal Theme** in the sidebar). You can change the neon accent colors of the portal to suit your preference or visibility needs (Cyan, Pink, Lime, Gold, Purple, Orange).
